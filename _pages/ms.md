- **Program**: Interdisciplinary Graduate Program in Medical Bigdata Convergence (빅데이터메디컬융합학과), College of IT, Kangwon National University
- **Lab**: Biomedical Data Science Lab (BDS Lab)
- **2023** — BK21 scholarship.
- **2023** — presented *톰슨 샘플링 기반의 신약 후보 물질 디자인을 위한 딥러닝 기법* (a deep learning method for drug candidate design based on Thompson sampling).
- **2024** — *Variational AutoEncoder with Mask-Guided Multi-Objective Optimization for De Novo Drug Design* (마스크 기반 다중 목적 최적화와 변분 오토인코더 기반의 신약 디자인), with Chihyun Park. A SELFIES-based VAE whose mask pooling layer learns which latent dimensions matter for each property, then reuses those odds as gradient weights during multi-objective optimization.
- **December 2024** — master's thesis.
