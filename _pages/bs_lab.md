I joined the **Biomedical Data Science Lab (BDS Lab)** as an undergraduate researcher around the summer break of my second year, in 2020. My work there was on deep generative models for de novo drug design.

## Timeline

- **Summer 2020** — joined BDS Lab as an undergraduate researcher.
- **October 2021** — first paper, at the Korea Database Conference (KDBC 2021): *Deep Learning Model to Generate Chemical Compounds with Drug Target Binding Affinity* (약물 표적 결합 친화력을 갖는 화합물을 생성하기 위한 딥러닝 모델), with Hyosoon Jang and Chihyun Park. A VAE trained jointly with a binding-affinity predictor, so that the latent space could be searched for compounds that bind BCL-2.
- **Fall 2022** — industry-academia project.
- **December 2022** — first-author journal paper in *DATABASE RESEARCH* 38(3), pp. 47–61: *A deep generative model for de novo drug design based on Thompson sampling* (톰슨 샘플링 기반의 신약 후보 물질 디자인을 위한 심층 생성 모델).
